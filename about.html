About Us


PT Dinamika Rekayasa Utama began its operations in 2010 under the initial name CV Dinamika Konstruksi. Since its inception, the company has served as a trusted partner to various State-Owned Enterprises (BUMN) and private companies, including PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk, PT Pembangunan Perumahan (Persero) Tbk, PT Pertamina (Persero), PT Perusahaan Gas Negara (PGN) Tbk, the Ministry of Public Works and Public Housing (PUPR), and others.


Our primary focus is providing consulting services and handling construction documentation for the various projects they manage. Driven by business growth and increasing client trust, we underwent a transformation on February 5, 2021, by restructuring the business entity into PT Dinamika Rekayasa Utama.


With over a decade of experience providing solutions for a wide range of structural issues—spanning buildings, bridges, and other infrastructure. PT Dinamika Rekayasa Utama remains committed to delivering quality services and innovative solutions, while contributing to the success of every client project.
