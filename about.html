About Us


PT Dinamika Rekayasa Utama began its business journey in 2010 under its original name, CV Dinamika Konstruksi. Since its establishment, the company has been committed to providing professional construction consulting and documentation services while building long-term partnerships founded on integrity, quality, and trust.


Through dedication and consistency in delivering outstanding services, PT Dinamika Rekayasa Utama has earned the trust of numerous State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs), government institutions, and leading private companies. Its clients include PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk, PT Pembangunan Perumahan (Persero) Tbk, PT Pertamina (Persero), PT Perusahaan Gas Negara (PGN) Tbk, the Ministry of Public Works and Housing (PUPR), as well as various other companies and institutions.


The company’s primary focus is to provide technical consulting and construction documentation services that support the successful execution of construction projects. By emphasizing accuracy, professionalism, and a thorough understanding of construction regulations, PT Dinamika Rekayasa Utama continuously strives to deliver effective solutions tailored to each client’s specific needs.


As the company expanded and gained increasing trust from its partners, it officially transformed its legal entity from CV Dinamika Konstruksi to PT Dinamika Rekayasa Utama on 5 February 2021. This milestone marked a significant step in broadening the company’s service portfolio, strengthening its business capabilities, and reinforcing its commitment to delivering higher standards of professional service.


With more than a decade of experience, PT Dinamika Rekayasa Utama has successfully addressed a wide range of structural challenges involving buildings, bridges, and other infrastructure projects. This extensive experience serves as a solid foundation for the company to continue growing, driving innovation, and creating lasting value for every project it undertakes.


Today, PT Dinamika Rekayasa Utama remains committed to becoming a trusted partner in the construction industry by delivering high-quality services, innovative solutions, and meaningful contributions to the success of every client’s project. Supported by competent professionals, rigorous quality standards, and a commitment to continuous improvement, the company is dedicated to creating sustainable value for all stakeholders.



Vision & Mission


Vision :

To become a leading construction consulting company recognised for excellence in service quality, innovation, professionalism, and integrity, while serving as a trusted partner in delivering sustainable, safe, and efficient infrastructure development that creates lasting value for all stakeholders.


Mission :

  • To provide professional, high-quality construction consulting services that prioritise client satisfaction while adhering to applicable technical standards and regulations.
  • To develop innovative, effective, and practical solutions tailored to the unique needs, challenges, and characteristics of every project.
  • To uphold the highest standards of integrity, professional ethics, and accountability throughout every stage of planning, supervision, and consulting services.
  • To continuously enhance the competence of our professionals through ongoing learning, technological advancement, and the development of expertise in the construction industry.
  • To contribute to sustainable infrastructure development by prioritising safety, quality, efficiency, and environmental responsibility, thereby creating a positive impact on society.
