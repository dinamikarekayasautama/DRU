Planning for the Campus Building Universitas Muhammadiyah Riau


Brief

The nine-story academic building at Universitas Muhammadiyah Riau (UMRI) is designed to accommodate instructional activities and general meeting spaces. It employs a dual structural system—combining a Special Moment Resisting Frame (SMRF) and shear walls—to enhance resistance against seismic loads. Steel is used for the roof construction, while the main structure utilizes reinforced concrete designed in accordance with safety and efficiency standards.
